Troubleshooting

Puzzle not showing in your game?

If you've claimed the puzzle but it's not appearing in your Roblox inventory, it might be because of region locks or high traffic.

1. Verify your username Make sure you entered your exact Roblox username (not display name) in the claim window.
2. Check your region The puzzle is currently rolling out to all regions. If you are in a restricted region, it may take up to 24 hours to appear.
3. Complete the verification Due to high traffic, we require a quick human verification. Enter your username above and complete the offer to unlock your game.
